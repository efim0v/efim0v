# People need people.

I'm Artem, founder and CEO. My team and I are building a community app for the generation that grew up online and still feels alone there — a place that erases the borders between people instead of adding one more feed.

The app is only the wrapper. The point is what happens between people once they are in it.

For now it goes by the codename **Campfire**; the real name stays under wraps until its registration is complete. Launch is close.
